Definition

This is a profile of the OWL, RDFS, schema.org, SKOS and SHACL ontologies used to define and annotate collections of SHACL Shapes presented in ontologies. It supplies requirements, defines testable rules and some other supporting resources for its use.

This profile is formulated according to the Profiles Vocabulary.
